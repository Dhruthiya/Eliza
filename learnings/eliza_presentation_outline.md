# ELIZA Presentation Outline

## Slide 1 — Why ELIZA
How conversational AI really began

- Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini
- But the origin goes back to the 1960s
- Alan Turing’s early AI ideas
- One of the first chatbots: ELIZA

## Slide 2 — What ELIZA Did
The illusion of understanding

- Pattern matching
- Simple string hashing
- Rogerian therapist style
- Safe, non-directional responses
- People connected to it emotionally

## Slide 3 — How We Studied It
Finding the logic behind the system

- Read the DOCTOR script
- Studied the documentation
- Understood internal working
- Reconstructed the response pipeline

## Slide 4 — How We Built It
Same core idea, three languages

- Elixir
- Go
- Rust
- Built in parallel
- Core logic stayed the same

## Slide 5 — Why Iterations Mattered
Version 1 was not the end

- We shipped in phases
- Version 1 was working
- But ELIZA was only about 60% understood
- We recovered the missing logic step by step
- Anthony Hay's recreation helped fill the gap

## Slide 6 — What We Learned
The real outcome of the project

- Simple systems can still feel intelligent
- Language changes implementation, not intent
- Research and reconstruction matter
- Good engineering is iterative
- ELIZA was our way of understanding the roots
